🧮 DeskCalc: The "Not-Yet-Casio"

Welcome to DeskCalc, the calculator that currently knows how to do basic math and nothing else.
💡 The Origin Story

I was told I couldn't build anything without AI holding my hand. So, I used AI to hold my hand while I built this. Take that, everyone!
🚀 What it can do (for now)

    Add, Subtract, Multiply, Divide: It does math, which is a big deal for a machine.

    Delete: Because we all make mistakes.

    Clear: Because sometimes you just need to start your life (and your math) over.

🛠️ The Tech Stack (The "I'm a Developer" part)

    HTML: The skeleton that keeps it from falling over.

    CSS: The fancy outfit that makes it look like it belongs on a screen.

    JavaScript: The brain that is currently O(n) in its slicing efficiency (yes, I know it's not optimal, don't @ me).
